Teacher plan · 25 minutes

Class: Grade 7 · Unit 2: Awesome animals · Lesson 2, part 2 (Activity Book, Team Talk)
Lesson aim: to read and understand a dialogue about animals.
Target language: Watch out! Wow! It’s so cute!
Materials: Unit 2 poster, Class Book page 43 dialogue (Emily, Bayan, Nawal and the hedgehog), Activity Book page 94–95, device or projector.

TimeStageWhat happensTeacher’s Book link
0–4Warm-up
Mission 1: Memory Match		Students flip cards to match animal body parts with words, as in the poster Memory game.“Starting the lesson”: play the Memory game with the Unit 2 poster.
4–10Formative 1
Mission 2: Listen and Complete		Drag the missing words into six sentences from the dialogue and say who says each one. Play audio 2.4 to check.Activity 1 (2.4): listen and complete the sentences. Who says them? Write. Then listen and check.
10–15Formative 2
Mission 3: True or False?		Decide T or F and find evidence in the dialogue. Check as a class.Activity 2: read the dialogue again and circle T or F. Explain your answers.
15–20Formative 3
Mission 4: Say It Right		Drag expressions into six short dialogues. Pairs practise reading them aloud.Activity 3 (2.5): read and complete the dialogues with the correct expressions.
20–25Closing
Mission 5: Fact or Fib?		Find the false sentence in two sets of animal facts. Challenge: write a dialogue. Exit ticket.Activity 4 and “Finishing the lesson”: Three facts and a fib. Summative questions.

Differentiation

  • Support 🌱: first-letter hints, the evidence line shown after each True/False, only two answer choices in Fact or Fib.
  • Standard 🌿: full activities.
  • Challenge 🌳: students must pick the evidence line before scoring, and write their own dialogue with Watch out! / Wow! / It’s so cute!

Answer keys

  • 2.4: 1 Emily: different · 2 Nawal: something · 3 Bayan: claws · 4 Nawal: wings · 5 Nawal: spines · 6 Emily: scared.
  • 2.5: 1 Wow! It’s so big! · 2 It’s so cute! · 3 Watch out! · 4 Watch out! · 5 Wow! · 6 It’s so cute!
  • Deaf/hard of hearing students: all tasks are visual and text-based; audio is optional.
